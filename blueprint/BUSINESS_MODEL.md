## 1. Business Model Overview

ECODrIx operates on a **"Services Fund SaaS"** model — agency services generate immediate cash flow while SaaS products scale in the background. This is the only viable bootstrap path for a solo founder without external funding.

```
PHASE 1: Services revenue → pays bills, funds SaaS build
PHASE 2: SaaS revenue → covers services team cost, founder draws salary
PHASE 3: SaaS > Services → transition to pure SaaS
PHASE 4: SaaS runs autonomously → location independence + freedom
```

## 6. Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Channel 1: Cold WhatsApp Outreach (Month 1–3)

```
Tool: LAIE itself (dogfooding)
Process:
  → Scrape 500 D2C brands + coaches in Mumbai/Bengaluru/Hyderabad
  → LAIE enriches with Instagram handle + business phone
  → Personalized WA message: "I noticed your Instagram has great products.
    I built a tool that auto-posts and captures leads — free 14-day trial?"
  → Conversion target: 5% → 25 trials from 500 messages
  → Trial to paid: 40% → 10 new customers per batch
Cost: ₹0 (outreach via client's WA or own number)
```

### Channel 2: LinkedIn Content (Month 1+, ongoing)

```
Positioning: "Tier 2 India founder building SaaS for Indian SMBs"
Content pillars:
  1. Behind-the-build (product progress, architecture decisions)
  2. Founder lessons (bootstrap, solo, discipline)
  3. Market insights (India SMB digital transformation)
  4. Product demos (screen recordings of ECODrIx features)
Frequency: 5 posts/week
Target: 5,000 followers by Month 6 → 2-3 leads/week from LinkedIn
```

### Channel 3: Instagram (Month 1+)

```
Format: Reels (AI voice, faceless)
Topics: "How I built WhatsApp automation for Indian businesses"
Frequency: 3 Reels/week (using LAIE content calendar — dogfooding)
Target: 1,000 followers by Month 3 → product-aware audience
```

## 7. Retention Strategy

### Product Stickiness

- WhatsApp CRM: once contacts + conversations are in ERIX, switching cost is high
- LAIE content calendar: 30-day forward calendar creates monthly engagement habit
- ERIX-FLOW: active automation flows create "I can't turn this off" dependency

### Engagement Programs

- **Weekly product tips** (WhatsApp + email): 1 power feature per week
- **Monthly ROI report**: auto-generated PDF showing "leads captured, messages sent, campaigns delivered"
- **Community:** WhatsApp group for ECODrIx customers (peer network = retention)
- **Quarterly business review:** For Scale + Enterprise — founder personally reviews client results

### Churn Recovery

- Day 0 cancellation: founder calls within 2 hours
- Offer: 1 month free + personal onboarding call
- Exit survey: mandatory 3-question form before cancellation
- Winback: 30-day email + WA sequence post-cancellation
