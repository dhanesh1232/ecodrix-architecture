## 1. GTM Strategy Overview

### The Constraint

- Solo founder (Dhanesh)
- Bootstrapped (₹0 external capital)
- Services revenue funds product development
- Time split: 60% product, 40% GTM

### The Strategy

**"Land and expand in one vertical, one city, then replicate."**

```
Month 1-2: Win 10 paying customers in Tirupati/AP (Real Estate + Education)
Month 3-4: Expand to adjacent cities (Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Chennai)
Month 5-6: Add 2nd vertical playbook (Healthcare, D2C)
Month 7-9: Launch partner program (agencies distribute)
Month 10-12: Product-led growth (free tier, self-serve, SEO traffic)
```

### GTM Funnel

```
                    AWARENESS (eyeballs)
                   /                     \
             Content                   Outreach
            (SEO, YT,                  (WhatsApp DMs,
             LinkedIn)                  local networks)
                   \                     /
                    INTEREST (visitors)
                          |

                          |
                    ACTIVATION (< 10 min to value)
                          |
                    CONVERSION (trial → paid)
                          |
                    RETENTION (weekly active)
                          |

```

## 7. Sales Process

### 7.1 Sales Playbook (Founder-Led)

```
Step 1: IDENTIFY (5 min)
├── Find SMB on Google Maps / IndiaMart / 99acres
├── Verify they're active on WhatsApp Business
└── Note: vertical, location, approximate size

Step 2: CONNECT (2 min)
├── Send personalized WhatsApp DM (see templates below)
├── No pitch — ask a question about their pain
└── Wait for reply (don't follow up same day)

Step 3: QUALIFY (5 min conversation)
├── How many leads/month?
├── How do you track follow-ups today?
├── What tools do you use (Excel/Tally/nothing)?
└── "Would it help if..." → bridge to demo

Step 4: DEMO (15 min)
├── Screen share OR send video
├── Show THEIR vertical template
├── Show the loop: lead → reply → pipeline → invoice → paid
└── "Want to try it free for 14 days?"

Step 5: TRIAL (14 days)
├── Set up their account (1-on-1 if first 10)
├── Connect their WhatsApp
├── Configure vertical template
├── Daily check-in for first 3 days
└── "Are you seeing value?" on day 7

Step 6: CLOSE (Day 10-14)
├── "Your trial ends in 4 days. Want to continue?"
├── Offer Founder's Plan (₹999 locked) for early adopters
└── Take payment via Razorpay link on WhatsApp (practice what we preach)
```

### 7.2 Outreach Templates

**Cold DM (Real Estate):**

```
Hi [Name], I see you work in real estate in [City].

Genuine question: when a lead messages you on WhatsApp, how do you make sure you follow up if you're busy with site visits?

I ask because I built a tool that does this automatically — captures every WhatsApp lead and follows up even when you can't. Used by brokers in AP.

Happy to show you in 3 minutes if you're curious. No pressure.
```

**Cold DM (Education):**

```
Hi [Name], I saw [Institute Name] on Google.

Quick question: during admission season, how many parent inquiries come on WhatsApp that you don't get to follow up on?

I built an automation tool specifically for coaching institutes — auto-replies with fee structure, books demo classes, and follows up with parents who said "I'll think about it."

Would a 3-minute demo be useful?
```

**Follow-up (Day 3, no reply):**

```
Hi [Name], just checking — did my last message make sense? No worries if it's not relevant for you. I'll stop here. 👍
```

### 7.3 Objection Handling

| Objection                              | Response                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "I don't need a CRM"                   | "Totally fair. This isn't really a CRM — it's WhatsApp automation. It just happens to track your leads too."             |
| "I'm happy with WhatsApp Business app" | "Great! This works ON TOP of WhatsApp. You keep your number, but now you have auto-replies, follow-ups, and a pipeline." |
| "It's too expensive"                   | "It's ₹999/mo — less than losing 2 leads per month. One closed deal pays for 2 years of the tool."                       |
| "I don't have time to learn"           | "Setup takes 5 minutes. I'll do it for you. You just reply to leads as usual — the automation runs in the background."   |
| "What if WhatsApp bans my number?"     | "We use the official Meta API — it's the same system HDFC and Swiggy use. Zero ban risk."                                |
| "I'll think about it"                  | "Of course. I'll share a 3-min video. Watch when free. If it clicks, just reply here."                                   |
