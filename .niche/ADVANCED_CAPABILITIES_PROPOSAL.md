## 1. Revenue Intelligence

**What no one offers:** Real-time visibility into where your money is coming from, where it's leaking, and what to do about it — without opening Tally or a spreadsheet.

### Features

| Feature                     | What it does                                                                        | Business impact                                 |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue per lead source** | "99acres brought ₹12L this quarter, Google Ads brought ₹3L"                         | Stop wasting money on bad channels              |
| **Deal velocity tracker**   | "Average deal takes 14 days in 'Negotiation' — 3x longer than 'Site Visit'"         | Find and fix pipeline bottlenecks               |
| **Lost deal analysis**      | "42% of lost deals cite 'went with competitor' — which competitor? On which deals?" | Counter-strategy against specific competitors   |
| **Revenue attribution**     | Which automation rule generated how much revenue?                                   | Prove ROI of every automation                   |
| **Cohort revenue**          | "January leads: ₹8L total revenue. March leads: ₹2L (still maturing)"               | Understand revenue maturity curves              |
| **Daily revenue pulse**     | "Today: ₹45,000 collected. This week: ₹2.1L. vs last week: +22%"                    | Owner knows the score without checking anything |

### Why No One Does This

- WATI/Interakt: No CRM, no revenue data
- Zoho/HubSpot: Requires manual dashboard setup + enterprise plan
- ECODrIx: **Automatic** — because we own the entire loop (lead source → deal → invoice → payment), attribution is free

## 3. Smart Follow-Up Engine

**What no one offers:** Automatically follows up with leads at the right time, in the right way, stopping when they respond — without the user setting up any automation.

### Features

| Feature                        | What it does                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Auto follow-up (no config)** | If a lead goes silent for 3 days, auto-send a gentle WhatsApp nudge. No rule setup required — works out of the box. |
| **Escalation**                 | Day 3: WhatsApp. Day 7: Email. Day 14: "This lead might be lost — call them?" notification to owner.                |
| **Smart timing**               | Send follow-ups when the lead is most likely to read (learned from their past read-time patterns).                  |
| **Stop on response**           | The moment they reply, all follow-ups cancel. No embarrassing double-messages.                                      |
| **Tone adaptation**            | First follow-up: friendly. Second: professional. Third: urgency. AI adapts tone.                                    |
| **Dead lead detection**        | After 3 failed follow-ups with no response, auto-move to "Cold" and stop bothering them.                            |

### Why This Is Unique

Every competitor requires you to BUILD the follow-up sequence manually. ECODrIx does it out of the box — zero configuration. The user's only action is "turn it on."

ECODrIx isn't a CRM you log into when you remember. It's an **intelligent business partner** that works 24/7 — following up with leads when you're busy, collecting payments when you forget, alerting you when something needs attention, scoring customers by their behavior, and telling you every morning what to focus on.
