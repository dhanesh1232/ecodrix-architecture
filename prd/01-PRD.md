# 01 — Product Requirements Document

> What ECODrIx is, who it's for, what it does, and what's explicitly out of scope.
> The roadmap (`06-ROADMAP.md`) decides _when_; this doc decides _what_.

## 1. Vision

**ECODrIx is an AI-native business operating system for Indian SMBs** — a single platform where
WhatsApp conversations, CRM pipelines, automated workflows, lead intelligence, and invoicing all
share one tenant boundary, one entitlement system, and one AI brain. The platform exists in three
shapes:

- A **self-serve SaaS** for direct customers (`ECOD/saas`)
- A **managed service** for freelance / agency-run accounts (`ECOD/admin`)
- An **embeddable platform** (`@ecodrix/erix-react` + `@ecodrix/erix-api`) so agencies can ship our CRM inside their clients' sites

The five-year arc: from a WhatsApp CRM with AI features (Year 1) → an AI agent that operates the
business (Year 2) → a customer-ops platform (Year 3) → a developer ecosystem and marketplace (Year 5).

## 3. Problem & Wedge

Indian SMBs lose 10+ hours a week to manual customer ops. Existing tools fail them:

- **Wati / Interakt / AiSensy** — messaging only, no CRM, no invoicing, no AI agent.
- **Zoho / HubSpot / Freshsales** — email-first, complex, ₹15–50k/mo, not WhatsApp-native.
- **Zapier / Make** — generic automation; users have to wire CRMs, WhatsApp tools, and invoice tools together.

ECODrIx wedges in by being **WhatsApp-native, AI-native, and bundled** at ₹2–12k/mo for India.

## 7. Data Architecture Decisions

### 7.1 Three table families, one Postgres

| Family      | Prefix      | Purpose                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform    | `ecodrix_*` | Orgs, users, plans, subscriptions, usage, audit, cloud storage                                                             |
| CRM         | `erix_*`    | Leads, conversations, pipelines, templates, broadcasts, workflows, invoices, automation, segments, meetings, notifications |
| Lead engine | `laie_*`    | 28 schemas covering tenants, actors, runs, datasets, audits, intelligence modules, leads, workflows                        |

All three families live in one Supabase Postgres. One `drizzle.config.ts` at `ECOD/server/drizzle.config.ts`.

### 7.2 Multi-source data modes

Each org chooses where its CRM data lives:

| Mode                 | Where                                                         | Adapter                               | Default for      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `platform`           | Shared Supabase, row-isolated by `org_id`                     | `PostgresAdapter`                     | direct           |
| `own` + `mongodb`    | Tenant's isolated Mongo (we provisioned or they pasted)       | `MongoAdapter` (wraps `getCrmModels`) | freelance        |
| `own` + `postgresql` | Tenant's Postgres with our schema                             | `PostgresAdapter` (per-tenant pool)   | enterprise       |
| `both`               | Postgres primary + sync to tenant store via `erix-sync` queue | `DualAdapter`                         | hybrid migration |

The boundary is the same regardless: every adapter call takes `orgId`. Only the factory knows which
backend to pick.

### 7.3 Tenant boundary

`tenantResolver` middleware loads `ecodrix_organizations` from `apiKey + clientCode` (SDK) or
NextAuth session (saas), attaches `req.org`. No query reaches the DB without it.

`requireFeature("path")` for booleans. Both rely on `tenantResolver` running first.

## 10. User Stories (canonical)

**US-1 (Direct signup → first lead)** — As Dr. Priya, I register at `ecodrix.com`, complete a 5-step onboarding wizard, connect WhatsApp, and capture my first inbound inquiry as a lead within 24 hours.

**US-2 (Freelance onboarding)** — As an agency operator, I add a new clinic from `ECOD/admin`, click "Provision MongoDB", attach a plan, and the clinic's data lands in their isolated Mongo while their entitlement is tracked on platform Postgres.

**US-3 (Plan upgrade)** — As Rahul, I hit my Free-tier 1k message limit; the SDK shows an UpgradePrompt; I click Upgrade, pay via Razorpay, and within 60 seconds my new limits are active and a Socket.io `entitlements:updated` event refreshes my UI.

**US-4 (Embeddable SDK)** — As Sneha, I install `@ecodrix/erix-react` in my client's React app, pass `apiKey + clientCode`, and her CRM dashboard renders inside the client's site with style-isolated dark theme.

**US-5 (Visual automation)** — As a coaching institute owner, I drag a `lead.created` trigger onto the canvas, connect it to an "AI qualify" node, an "If score > 70" condition, and a "Move to Qualified stage" action; I activate the workflow and the next inbound lead is automatically qualified and moved.

**US-6 (LAIE audit → ERIX lead)** — As a marketing manager, I search "Apollo Diagnostics, Mumbai" in LAIE, see a 90-second audit, click "Push to ERIX with outreach kit", and a new lead appears in my pipeline with the kit attached as the first activity.

**US-7 (Mode switch)** — As an enterprise admin, I switch from `platform` mode to `both` mode in `/settings/data-source`; existing data stays in Postgres, new writes mirror to my external Mongo, and I can verify with a smoke test before fully cutting over.
